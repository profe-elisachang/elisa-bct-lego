## 1
title: Substitution drills
content: Practice saying "Nǐ hǎo!" with different time expressions: zǎoshang (早上), xiàwǔ (下午), wǎnshang (晚上), nímen (你們)

## 2
title: Complete the following dialogue
instruction: Mr Wang and Miss Li are meeting for the first time. What should they say?
content: Use the patterns you have learned: nǐ hǎo (你好), wǎnshang hǎo (晚上好), zǎoshang hǎo (早上好)

## 3
title: Speech scenario
instruction: Mr Wang is going to give a speech at a party this evening. How should he begin?
content: Nǚshìmen, xiānshengmen, péngyoumen, wǎnshang hǎo! (女士們，先生們，朋友們，晚上好！)
