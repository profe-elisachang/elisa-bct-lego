Lesson 3: 我來介紹一下

👨‍💼 Mr. Wang:
您好，您貴姓？
Nín hǎo, nín guìxìng?
Hello, what is your surname?
👩‍💼 Ms. Li:
我姓李，您貴姓？
Wǒ xìng Lǐ, nín guìxìng?
My surname is Li, and you?
